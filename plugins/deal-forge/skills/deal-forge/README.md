# Deal Forge

- Finds everyone you've texted, sorts them (past clients, leads, referral partners, agents, vendors, friends,
  family), drafts a short reconnect text for each, and shows who to reach out to today.
- Runs on your Mac with your own Claude plan (Pro is usually enough) and your own GoHighLevel.
- Nothing is ever sent for you: "Open in Messages" fills the text, you press send.
- **Full install and how-to guide:** https://github.com/dealsmithrealty/dsr-agent-kit#readme
- Quick version, inside Claude Code:
  - `/plugin marketplace add dealsmithrealty/dsr-agent-kit`
  - `/plugin install deal-forge@dsr-agent-kit`, then restart Claude Code
  - `/forge-setup` once, then `/forge` every week
